PuzzleLoo – Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 29, 2026

PuzzleLoo is a single-player offline puzzle game. We built it to respect your privacy.

Data we collect

None. PuzzleLoo does not collect, transmit, sell or share any personal information. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no third-party tracking SDKs.

Data stored on your device

Your game progress (solved levels, best times, stars) and settings (sound, vibration, language) are saved only on your device. They never leave it. Deleting the app or using “Reset all progress” in Settings removes this data.

Children

PuzzleLoo is suitable for all ages and does not knowingly collect data from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published on this page.

Contact

Questions? Contact the developer via the support link on the App Store or Google Play listing.
