## Full description (EN)
Choose. Connect. Solve!

PuzzleLoo is a relaxing yet clever logic puzzle. Drag a line from each glowing shape to its matching twin. Lines can't cross, and every square on the board must be filled.

• 120 hand-checked levels across 5 chapters, from 5×5 to 9×9
• A fresh Daily Puzzle every day
• Colour + shape pairs, so it's colour-blind friendly
• Hints when you're stuck, undo, reset and a timer to beat your best
• Earn up to 3 stars per level
• English and Arabic
• Works fully offline
• No ads. No subscription. No data collected.
